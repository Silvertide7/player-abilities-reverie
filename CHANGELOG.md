1.0.4
---
- Rebalance ability cooldowns. Most abilities come back noticeably faster than before.
- Canopy Leap (4/3/2 min), Excavate (8/6/4 min) and Restful Meditation (15/12/10 min) now shorten as they level up.
- Deepsight and Fathom's Eye now scale the other way (5/8/15 min), since higher levels grant a much longer vision duration.
